Share your screen

With sound, the stream sends what the microphone hears all the time (no push-to-talk), so viewers hear the surroundings. It pauses while you talk to the channel and while your microphone is muted. Headphones keep friends' voices out of it. Keep this page open with the screen on (a phone pauses the camera otherwise); 🔄 switches between the back and front cameras while streaming. On mobile data, every viewer is a separate upload: about 0.45 GB per hour per viewer at 480p, 0.9 GB at 720p.

Sound comes only from a tab (switch on "Also share tab audio") or an entire screen (switch on "Also share system audio"), e.g. for a game. A single window has no sound. Your friends' voices from this page are left out, so they don't hear themselves; don't run the desktop TeamSpeak client at the same time.

The resolution is a ceiling: a smaller window keeps its own size and shape. From 2.5 Mbit/s (720p 30) to 10 Mbit/s (4K 60) per viewer, and every viewer is a separate upload from your computer. The video goes straight to the viewers (or through the relay, if your network needs it). Works in Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera…) and in Firefox (without sound).
